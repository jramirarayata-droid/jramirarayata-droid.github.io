Privacy Policy

Clover Hollow Tales and the "Clover Hollow Uploader" app · Last updated October 29, 2026

Who we are

Clover Hollow Tales is a YouTube channel of short animated stories for young children. "Clover Hollow Uploader" is a private tool used only by the channel's owner to publish the channel's own videos.

What the uploader app does

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel, and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists and scheduled publish times. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.

What data we access

  • Through Google sign-in, the app accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account, for the purposes above.
  • The app does not access, collect or store any information about viewers, children or other YouTube users.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • The only stored credential is the owner's own access token, kept in a private location under the owner's control.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Viewers and children

Our videos are marked "Made for Kids" on YouTube. We do not collect personal information from viewers. Watching our videos on YouTube is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Access granted to the uploader app can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
